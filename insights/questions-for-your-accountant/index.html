Five questions worth asking your accountant

A strong working relationship starts with conversations that go beyond the annual deadline.

FORMA JOURNALEDITORIAL SAMPLE · FOR APPROVAL

Useful accounting conversations are grounded in context: what you want to achieve, what has changed, and what may be coming next.

What is changing in my business?

Share growth plans, staffing changes, financing needs and operational concerns so discussions relate to your decisions.

Which numbers should I watch?

Choose a small set of meaningful measures that reflect your business model, rather than a dashboard of everything.

What should we prepare for?

Ask which reporting, tax and administrative responsibilities need attention in the coming months.
