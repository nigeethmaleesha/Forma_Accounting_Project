THE JOURNAL / Business

A clearer approach to cash flow

Understanding what moves in and out of the business is a strong starting point for better decisions.

FORMA JOURNALEDITORIAL SAMPLE · FOR APPROVAL

Cash flow provides a view of the timing of money coming in and going out. It is not the same as profit, and both measures matter.

Look beyond your bank balance

A healthy bank balance today does not always mean future bills are covered. Compare expected receipts with upcoming outgoings over a practical time horizon.

Create a repeatable rhythm

Regular, simple reviews can help spot seasonal trends, late invoices and large one-off commitments before they become surprises.

Use forecasts as conversations

A forecast is a working estimate rather than a guarantee. Review assumptions and revise it when circumstances change.
